# 英语启蒙 App 出图提示词（79 张）

产品经理 · 2026-10-25 · 适用于 Gemini 3.1 Flash Image
共 6 批：反义词单图 24 张，「说一说」L3 15 张、L4 22 张、L5 8 张、L6 3 张、L7 7 张。

## 怎么用

1. **每张图开一个新对话。** 在同一个对话里连着画，模型会照着上一张改，构图会越来越像。
2. 每次粘贴两段：**先贴【固定开头】，紧接着贴这张图的那一条**，中间空一格就行。
3. 如果能上传参考图，就把 `refs/01_人物头像.jpg` 一起传上去，人物长相会更一致。
4. 画好后按条目上的文件名保存，比如 `adj_big.jpg`，放进 `out/` 文件夹。
5. 建议先画第 1、2 批，这两批最急。

## 画完自查

有一项不合格就重画一次。还不行就跳过，记下文件名告诉产品经理。

1. **人数对：** 和这一条最后写的名单一致，不能多画人。
2. **没有字：** 图里没有任何文字、字母、数字。
3. **没有边框：** 画面铺满整张图。
4. **长相对：**
   - Tom：红 T 恤、红短裤，头顶一撮翘发；
   - Amy：黄衣服，两个**短**辫子，不是麻花辫；
   - 妈妈：粉色开衫；
   - 老师：浅绿衬衫加黄色围裙。
5. **物品没有脸：** 太阳、云、灯泡、食物这些都不能画脸。

## 【固定开头】每张都先贴这一段

```
Generate an image. STYLE: Flat 2D children's picture-book illustration, clean dark-brown outlines of even thickness, flat colors with no gradients, no 3D shading, no photorealism, warm off-white paper background with subtle paper texture. Full-bleed illustration, no border, no frame, no inset card, no rounded-corner panel. Square 1:1. Main subject fills about 65% of the frame, centered, with simple uncluttered background. Characters have chibi proportions: big round head, short body, friendly expressions. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos anywhere (signs, books, badges, labels and screens are blank). Objects, food, plants, the sun, the moon and clouds have no faces; only real people and real animals have faces. CHARACTERS (use only the ones the scene names): TOM: a 5-year-old Chinese boy, round face, short black hair with one small upright tuft on top, big dark-brown eyes with white sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, red crew-neck T-shirt, red shorts, white socks, red-and-white sneakers. MOM: Tom's mother, a young Chinese woman, oval face, long straight black hair with a side part falling past her shoulders, small earrings, pink cheeks, a long open pink cardigan over a plain white top, dark-blue trousers. DAD: Tom's father, a young Chinese man, round face, short neat black hair, black rectangular glasses, light-blue collared shirt, dark-grey trousers. TEACHER: a young Chinese female kindergarten teacher, chin-length black bob haircut with side-swept bangs, pink cheeks, a light-green collared short-sleeve shirt with a yellow bib apron over it, dark trousers; she looks clearly different from Mom. GRANDMA: a kind elderly Chinese woman, short curly silver-grey hair, purple button-up cardigan over a cream top, dark skirt. GRANDPA: a kind elderly Chinese man, bald on top with white hair at the sides, round gold-rimmed glasses, brown knitted sweater vest over a cream collared shirt. FARMER: a cheerful middle-aged Chinese man with tanned skin, wide woven straw hat, blue short-sleeve work shirt, white towel around his neck, rubber boots. STAFF: a friendly young Chinese woman worker, olive-green cap, black low ponytail, olive-green work shirt with two chest pockets. CLASSMATES: other 5-year-old children with varied hairstyles, wearing blue, green, purple or orange (none wears a red T-shirt or a yellow skirt). BROTHER: Tom's 9-year-old big brother, one head taller than Tom, short black hair, green T-shirt, blue jeans. BUS DRIVER: a middle-aged Chinese woman, short black hair, navy uniform and navy cap. SCHOOLGIRL: an 8-year-old Chinese girl in a school uniform (white shirt, navy skirt) with a backpack. Only the characters named in the scene appear. Nobody else. Never write the meaning words in the image.
```
