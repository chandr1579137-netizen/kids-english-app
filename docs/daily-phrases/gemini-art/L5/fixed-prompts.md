# 固定描述（L5 使用版）

2026-10-26。L5 课程插图使用统一的人物外形、风格定义与负向约束。

## 统一描述结构
每张图开头写 "Generate an image."，接 STYLE、登场角色设定、收尾句，再接 SCENE。

### STYLE
```
STYLE: Flat 2D children's picture-book illustration, clean dark-brown outlines, flat colors with no gradients or 3D shading, warm off-white paper background with subtle texture. Full-bleed illustration, no border, no frame, no inset card. Square 1:1. Main subject fills about 65% of the frame. Characters have chibi proportions: big round head, short body. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere (signs, badges, books, boards are blank). Objects and animals have no faces unless they are real animals.
```

### 角色设定
- **TOM**：a 5-year-old Chinese boy, round face, short black hair with one small upright tuft on top, big dark-brown eyes with white sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, red crew-neck T-shirt, red shorts, white socks, red-and-white sneakers.
- **AMY**：a 5-year-old Chinese girl, round face, black hair with straight bangs and two short low pigtails (not braids) tied with yellow bows, big dark-brown eyes with sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, yellow T-shirt, yellow skirt, white socks, yellow Mary Jane shoes.
- **MOM**：Tom's mother, a young Chinese woman, oval face, long straight black hair falling past her shoulders with a side part, small earrings, dark-brown eyes with sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, a long open pink cardigan over a plain white top, dark-blue trousers.
- **DAD**：Tom's father, a young Chinese man, round face, short neat black hair, black rectangular glasses, friendly smile, light-blue collared shirt, dark-grey trousers.
- **BROTHER**：Tom's big brother, a 9-year-old Chinese boy about one head taller than Tom, short neat black hair, green T-shirt, blue jeans, big friendly grin.
- **GRANDMA**：Tom's grandmother, a kind elderly Chinese woman, short curly silver-grey hair, gentle wrinkles, pink cheeks, purple button-up cardigan over a cream top, dark skirt.
- **STAFF**：a friendly young Chinese woman worker, olive-green cap, black hair in a low ponytail, olive-green work shirt with two chest pockets, dark trousers, warm smile.

### 负向约束（铁律）
- **零文字**：严禁出现任何字母（包括名字标牌、字母挂图、商品标签、台签字迹、鞋盒字样）、数字、条形码或伪符号。
- **满幅画面**：无外边框、无内嵌画框、无四周白边卡片效果。
- **非生命物体绝无脸**：雪人（除规定胡萝卜鼻和煤球眼外绝无拟人嘴巴表情）、太阳、云朵、植物、雨伞、器物绝无拟人五官。
