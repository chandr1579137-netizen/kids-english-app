# 固定描述（L7 使用版）

2026-10-26。L7 课程插图使用统一的人物外形、风格定义与负向约束。

## 统一描述结构
每张图开头写 "Generate an image."，接 STYLE、登场角色设定、收尾句，再接 SCENE。

### STYLE
```
STYLE: Flat 2D children's picture-book illustration, clean dark-brown outlines, flat colors with no gradients or 3D shading, warm off-white paper background with subtle texture. Full-bleed illustration, no border, no frame, no inset card. Square 1:1. Main subject fills about 65% of the frame. Characters have chibi proportions: big round head, short body. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere (signs, badges, books, boards, tickets are blank or pure pictures). No speech bubbles. Objects, light bulbs, decorations and picnic blankets have no faces.
```

### 角色设定
- **TOM**：a 5-year-old Chinese boy, round face, short black hair with one small upright tuft on top, big dark-brown eyes with white sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, red crew-neck T-shirt, red shorts, white socks, red-and-white sneakers.
- **DAD**：Tom's father, an East Asian man in his 30s, neat short black hair, black rectangular-frame glasses, gentle smile, light-blue button-up shirt, dark-grey trousers.
- **MOM**：Tom's mother, an East Asian woman in her 30s, gentle smile, shoulder-length black hair, pink open long cardigan over a cream top, dark-blue trousers.
- **GRANDPA**：Tom's grandfather, a kind elderly Chinese man, short grey hair, round wire-rim glasses, gentle smile, brown knitted vest over a collared shirt.
- **STAFF**：a friendly female zoo ticketing staff, warm smile, simple uniform behind the window counter.

### 负向约束（铁律）
- **绝对零文字**：门票上仅纯动物小插画，绝无字母、英文单词或数字；售票窗标牌空白无字；行李箱无标签文字；绝无对话气泡（no speech bubbles）；绝无拟声词。
- **满幅画面**：无外边框、无内嵌画框、无四周大白边卡片效果。
- **非生命物体绝无脸**：小灯泡（light bulb）仅发光灯丝、野餐垫、野餐篮、三明治、水果、水杯、窗户、飞机绝无拟人化卡通脸。
