# 固定描述（L6 使用版）

2026-10-26。L6 课程插图使用统一的人物外形、风格定义与负向约束。

## 统一描述结构
每张图开头写 "Generate an image."，接 STYLE、登场角色设定、收尾句，再接 SCENE。

### STYLE
```
STYLE: Flat 2D children's picture-book illustration, clean dark-brown outlines, flat colors with no gradients or 3D shading, warm off-white paper background with subtle texture. Full-bleed illustration, no border, no frame, no inset card. Square 1:1. Main subject fills about 65% of the frame. Characters have chibi proportions: big round head, short body. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere (signs, badges, books, boards are blank). Objects, decorations, lanterns and balloons have no faces.
```

### 角色设定
- **TOM**：a 5-year-old Chinese boy, round face, short black hair with one small upright tuft on top, big dark-brown eyes with white sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, red crew-neck T-shirt, red shorts, white socks, red-and-white sneakers.
- **AMY**：a 5-year-old Chinese girl, round face, black hair with straight bangs and two short low pigtails (not braids) tied with yellow bows, big dark-brown eyes with sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, yellow T-shirt, yellow skirt, white socks, yellow Mary Jane shoes.
- **GRANDMA**：Tom's grandmother, a kind elderly Chinese woman, short curly silver-grey hair, gentle wrinkles, pink cheeks, purple button-up cardigan over a cream top, dark skirt.
- **CLASSMATES**：other 5-year-old kindergarten children with varied hairstyles and clothes in blue, green, purple or orange (none of them wears a red T-shirt or a yellow skirt, so Tom and Amy stay easy to spot).

### 负向约束（铁律）
- **零文字**：红灯笼为素面纯红，绝无“福”、“春”等汉字；红包仅为金色花纹，绝无“利是”、“福”等字样；吊旗与碎纸屑绝无字母数字。
- **满幅画面**：无外边框、无内嵌画框、无四周白边卡片效果。
- **非生命物体绝无脸**：红灯笼、气球、烟花、门窗、器物绝无拟人化笑脸。
