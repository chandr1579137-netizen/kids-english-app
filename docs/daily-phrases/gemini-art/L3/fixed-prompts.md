# 固定描述（L3 使用版）

2026-10-26。L3 课程插图使用统一的人物外形、风格定义与负向约束。

## 统一描述结构
每张图开头写 "Generate an image."，接 STYLE、登场角色设定、收尾句，再接 SCENE。

### STYLE
```
STYLE: Flat 2D children's picture-book illustration, clean dark-brown outlines, flat colors with no gradients or 3D shading, warm off-white paper background with subtle texture. Full-bleed illustration, no border, no frame, no inset card. Square 1:1. Main subject fills about 65% of the frame. Characters have chibi proportions: big round head, short body. No text, no letters, no numbers anywhere (signs, badges, books, boards are blank). Objects and animals have no faces unless they are real animals.
```

### 角色设定
- **TOM**：a 4-year-old Chinese boy, round face, short black hair with one small upright tuft on top, big dark-brown eyes with white sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, plain red crew-neck T-shirt, red shorts, white socks, red-and-white sneakers.
- **AMY**：a 4-year-old Chinese girl, round face, black hair with straight bangs and two short low pigtails (not braids) tied with yellow bows, big dark-brown eyes with sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, plain yellow A-line dress, white socks, yellow Mary Jane shoes.
- **MOM**：Tom's mother, a young Chinese woman, oval face, long straight black hair falling past her shoulders with a side part, small earrings, dark-brown eyes with sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, a long open pink cardigan over a plain white top, dark-blue trousers.
- **DAD**：Tom's father, a young Chinese man, short black hair, black-rimmed glasses, gentle eyes with sparkle highlights, light-blue long-sleeve shirt, grey trousers, dark slippers.
- **TEACHER**：a young Chinese female kindergarten teacher, round friendly face, chin-length black bob haircut with side-swept bangs, dark-brown eyes with sparkle highlights, pink cheeks, a light-green collared shirt with a plain pastel yellow apron over it, dark trousers, flat black shoes.

### 负向约束（铁律）
- **零文字**：严禁出现任何字母（包括名字标牌、字母挂图、黑板字迹、书脊字样）、数字、条形码或标签。
- **满幅画面**：无外边框、无内嵌画框、无四周白边卡片效果。
